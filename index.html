01

The Human Bottleneck

GTM analysts spent 36 hours every month manually compiling master-data reports and checking thousands of customer records. Simultaneously, the Tax team spent over 25 hours reconciling SAP ledgers against the FBR portal line by line. Skilled operators were trapped in copy-paste loops where fatigue invited costly compliance errors.

02

In-Memory Architecture

Instead of fragile worksheet formulas that freeze corporate laptops, I engineered seven tools running entirely in RAM using Scripting.Dictionary hash tables and dynamic arrays. I packaged the suite into portable .xlam add-ins with a custom XML ribbon and a 4-phase visual guide so any supervisor could install it in under a minute without IT tickets.

03

Executive Adoption & The 7 Engines

The suite cut GTM reporting down to under 15 minutes (saving 33 net hours/month) and compressed a 26-hour Tax reconciliation into 192 seconds (saving 26.4 net hours/month). After presenting the ROI to the COO and CFO, the tools remained in permanent production across both departments.
