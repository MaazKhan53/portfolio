Problem

GTM spent 36 hours a month on manual reporting and master-data checks. Tax spent about 25 hours reconciling SAP against the FBR dashboard line by line. Skilled people were doing copy and paste, and errors crept in.

Problem

Auditing content for ranking opportunity by hand does not scale, and a model that flatters itself is worse than no model.

Solution

Logistic regression and random forest models validated with client-grouped splits so no client appears in both train and test, a leakage harness, drift monitoring, and claims framed under the NIST AI RMF.

Result

Grouped validation exposed client memorization, and I reported the lower, honest number. An engineered leakage feature scored 100% accuracy, then 48.5% once removed. Completed with a verified FlyRank credential.
